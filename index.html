Pawn Shop Kit

65 interaction-ready props, fixtures and room pieces. Every door, drawer, lid and dial is its own part, pivoted where it really moves, with colliders and ready-to-use scripts.

Coming soon to the Unity Asset Store Read the documentation

Built for games where players touch things

Moving parts, ready to use

25 hinges, slides and spins across 16 models. Call Toggle() from your raycast, or SetOpenAmount(0..1) for drag-to-open.

Holders that hold

Pick up the ring tray and the rings come with it. Guitars go back on the pegboard or into their case, and a necklace drapes on its display bust.

Physics-ready props

Real-world mass on every pickup and a centre of mass over its base, so dropped items land the right way up.

Walk-in demo scene

A furnished shop and back office using all 65 prefabs, with a first-person controller: open, pick up, put back, crouch.

Clean and consistent

Real-world scale, wall-snapping origins, seamless PBR textures at 1024 px/m, lightmap UVs and 3-level LODs on heavy models.

Built-in, URP and Godot

Built-in materials out of the box, one-click conversion to URP, and Godot 4 GLBs with an import script.
